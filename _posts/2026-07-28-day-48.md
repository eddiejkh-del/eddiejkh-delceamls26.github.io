what_i_learned: |
  Overall, throughout my experience with CEAMLS, I have learned far more than I expected, both academically and personally. I strengthened my Python programming skills and gained a much deeper understanding of machine learning by applying it to real-world environmental research. Through our PM2.5 project, I learned about the relationship between PM2.5, aerosols, air quality, and how these factors affect both the environment and public health. I also developed valuable research skills by analyzing data, writing a research paper in Overleaf, and presenting our findings to others. These experiences showed me how engineering, computer science, and environmental science can work together to solve meaningful problems. Every challenge throughout the program became an opportunity to learn something new and improve my technical and critical thinking abilities.

  Beyond the technical knowledge, one of the most meaningful parts of CEAMLS was experiencing the diversity of Morgan State University as a host institution. I had the opportunity to meet and collaborate with students from different backgrounds, including someone from Zimbabwe, which gave me a broader perspective and allowed me to learn from experiences different from my own. Working alongside so many talented and motivated people taught me the importance of teamwork, communication, and sharing ideas to find solutions together. I also formed friendships and created memories that I will carry with me long after the program has ended. Looking back, I feel incredibly honored and grateful to have been selected as a participant in CEAMLS and to have been part of such a supportive and inspiring community. Overall, this experience has prepared me academically, professionally, and personally, and it has given me lasting friendships, unforgettable memories, and skills that will benefit me throughout my future career.
