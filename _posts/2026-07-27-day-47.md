what_i_learned: |
  Overall, throughout the summer I learned a tremendous amount about programming, machine learning, and conducting research. One of the most valuable skills I developed was learning Python and how to use it to process data, build machine learning models, and analyze results. I also gained a much deeper understanding of machine learning, particularly the differences between linear models, tree-based models, and ensemble models, along with the strengths and limitations of each approach. Working with these models taught me how to evaluate their performance using metrics such as R² and RMSE while improving predictions through feature engineering techniques like lagging, rolling, and mutual information. In addition, I became familiar with several Python libraries, including pandas for data manipulation, NumPy for numerical computing, Matplotlib for data visualization, scikit-learn for machine learning, and XGBoost, LightGBM, and CatBoost for advanced ensemble learning. Learning how these libraries work together gave me the ability to build complete machine learning workflows from data preparation to model evaluation.

  Another major accomplishment was learning how to write and organize a research paper using Overleaf and LaTeX. Throughout the summer, I improved my technical writing skills by creating literature reviews, formatting citations, designing tables and figures, and presenting results in a professional research format. I also learned the importance of collaboration by working with my team to develop our PM2.5 prediction project, share ideas, and solve problems together. Beyond the technical skills, I gained experience presenting my work, creating demonstration videos, and communicating complex ideas to others in a clear and organized manner. Looking back, I realized that this experience helped me grow not only as a programmer and researcher but also as a critical thinker and problem solver. Overall, this summer provided me with valuable technical knowledge, research experience, and practical skills that will benefit me in future academic projects and my engineering career.
