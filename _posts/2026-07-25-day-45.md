what_i_learned: |
  Today I learned how applications such as Google Vids, App Shortcuts, Measurement, and Maker are being improved through the development of artificial intelligence. These AI-powered tools are making it easier to create videos, automate repetitive tasks, measure objects more accurately, and assist with creative projects. Learning about these advancements showed me how AI is becoming integrated into everyday applications to improve productivity and efficiency. I also explored VeltConnect and learned how it incorporates different machine learning techniques to analyze and organize data. One example was the use of Random Forest for classification tasks, where the model predicts categories based on patterns learned from labeled data. I also learned that Linear Regression is used to predict continuous numerical values by modeling the relationship between variables.

   In addition, I learned about K-means clustering, which is an unsupervised machine learning technique. Unlike supervised learning methods, K-means does not require labeled data and instead groups similar data points into clusters based on their characteristics. The algorithm automatically identifies patterns within the dataset and assigns each data point to the cluster that best fits it. Alongside these AI concepts, I also explored a website designed to teach K–12 students the four forces of flight: lift, drag, thrust, and weight, and how these forces work together to keep an airplane in the air. The interactive lessons explained how airplanes are designed and built by applying these engineering principles, making complex topics easier to understand. Overall, today’s learning demonstrated how artificial intelligence and interactive educational tools are making both technology and engineering concepts more accessible to learners of all ages.
